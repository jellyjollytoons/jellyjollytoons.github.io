Privacy policy

Chani AOS. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who we are

Chani AOS is a private desktop tool that the owner of the JellyJolly Toons YouTube channel uses to upload their own videos. It runs on the owner's own computer. It has no other users and no public sign-up. Contact: JellyJollyToons@gmail.com.

What Google data we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Chani AOS asks for:

  • Your email address and basic profile (openid, email), to show which Google account is connected.
  • Upload videos (youtube.upload), to upload videos the owner has approved to their own channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly), to read the channel name, its ID and the status of uploaded videos.
  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.force-ssl), to set the title, description, thumbnail, playlist and publish time of the owner's own videos.

How we use it

This data is used only to publish and manage videos on the owner's own channel, and only when the owner has approved each video. Chani AOS does not read, change or delete anything else in the account.

Where it is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any server run by us, and no copy is kept anywhere else. Video data goes only to YouTube through Google's official YouTube Data API.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train any AI or machine learning model.

Chani AOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Chani AOS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes it from Chani AOS.

Children

JellyJolly Toons makes videos for children, but children do not use Chani AOS and it collects no data about viewers. Viewers' data is handled by YouTube under Google's own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
