Chani AOS

A private publishing tool for the JellyJolly Toons YouTube channel.

What it does

Chani AOS is a desktop tool used by the owner of JellyJolly Toons, a channel of songs and short animated videos for young children. When the owner approves a finished video, Chani AOS uploads it to the JellyJolly Toons YouTube channel with its title, description and schedule.

Who can use it

Chani AOS is not a public app. It runs only on the owner's own computer and is used only by the owner to manage channels they own. It does not offer accounts to anyone else.

Google sign-in

Chani AOS asks the channel owner to sign in with Google so it can upload videos to their own YouTube channel and read that channel's basic details. Read the privacy policy to see exactly what it uses and why.
